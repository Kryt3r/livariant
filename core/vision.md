# Vision

> **Projects should accumulate wisdom, not entropy.**

We envision a future where software projects accumulate wisdom instead of entropy.

Projects should remain coherent, understandable, secure, intentionally designed, and trustworthy throughout their entire lifetime—regardless of how many people, AI agents, tools, or years contribute to them.

The Project Brain Framework exists to preserve not only code, but also the reasoning, design decisions, architecture, engineering standards, and product identity that give software lasting coherence.

Software should age through accumulated knowledge—not accumulated chaos.

## Interpretation

The framework treats software entropy as a first-class problem.

Entropy appears when:
- decisions lose their rationale,
- documentation diverges from implementation,
- architecture drifts without intent,
- design identity becomes generic or inconsistent,
- security assumptions erode,
- new contributors or agents are forced to guess,
- project context becomes fragmented across chats, tools, or memories.

The framework exists to counter this drift by preserving useful knowledge, maintaining clear ownership, surfacing contradictions, enforcing risk-aware process, and making important decisions discoverable.

The vision is not maximum process, maximum documentation, or maximum automation.

The vision is durable coherence.
