# Mission

The Project Brain Framework helps humans and AI agents build and maintain complex software without losing context, intent, quality, or product identity over time.

It does this by combining persistent project knowledge with risk-aware engineering workflows, explicit decision records, security and quality gates, design intelligence, model-aware task routing, and modular governance that survives changes in tools, agents, teams, and technology.

The framework turns isolated AI-assisted development sessions into a continuous engineering process in which knowledge compounds instead of being repeatedly rediscovered.

It is designed to remain lightweight where possible, strict where necessary, and adaptable to different project types without sacrificing its core principles.

**Its mission is not to make AI write more code. Its mission is to make AI-assisted software development more coherent, deliberate, and sustainable.**

The framework strengthens human ownership by making AI reasoning, recommendations, assumptions, and changes easier to inspect, challenge, and preserve.

## Mission Pillars

### Preserve

Preserve relevant knowledge, intent, rationale, decisions, and lessons so projects do not repeatedly rediscover what they already learned.

### Guide

Guide humans and AI agents through risk-aware workflows that are proportionate to the task instead of imposing the same process everywhere.

### Verify

Verify security, quality, and completion through evidence, testing, review, and reproducible checks rather than agent self-assertion.

### Evolve

Allow projects, teams, tools, and models to change without forcing the organizational memory and engineering discipline of the project to reset.
