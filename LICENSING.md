# Livariant Licensing

Livariant is **source-available** software. It is not offered as OSI-approved Open Source.

## Default license

Unless a file or component says otherwise, Livariant is licensed under the **PolyForm Perimeter License 1.0.1**. The authoritative license terms are in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

PolyForm Perimeter is designed to permit use, modification, and distribution while excluding use of the software to provide others with a product that competes with the software itself.

## What this means in practice

Livariant is intended to remain broadly usable by individuals, teams, and companies, including in commercial software-development work.

Examples of intended permitted use include:

- using Livariant to build or maintain your own commercial software;
- using Livariant internally within a company;
- inspecting and learning from the source;
- modifying Livariant for a permitted purpose;
- distributing permitted modifications subject to the license terms.

The default license is not intended to permit taking Livariant, a fork of Livariant, or a product built from Livariant and offering it to others as a substitute for Livariant's functionality or value.

The license text, not this summary, controls whether a particular use is permitted.

## Commercial licenses

The Livariant rights holder may offer separate commercial license terms for uses that are not permitted by the default PolyForm Perimeter license, or for organizations that want separately negotiated rights, warranties, support, embedding/OEM rights, or other commercial arrangements.

Nothing in the default license prevents the rights holder from licensing software they have authority to license under different terms.

## Community and commercial development

The licensing model is intended to preserve both of these goals:

1. Livariant should be useful to a broad developer community, including developers working on commercial projects.
2. The Livariant project should retain the ability to build a sustainable commercial product without granting an unrestricted right to repackage Livariant into a competing offering.

This boundary should remain explicit. Livariant must not be marketed as OSI Open Source while the competition restriction applies.

## Contributions

External code contributions are not yet accepted for incorporation into Livariant until contributor terms are finalized.

This temporary gate exists to avoid accidentally creating incompatible ownership or relicensing constraints before the project's long-term contributor and commercial-licensing model is established.

Bug reports, design discussion, documentation feedback, and other non-code participation may still be welcomed according to `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

Before external code contributions are opened, the project will publish the applicable contributor terms. Those terms must be reviewed for compatibility with the source-available license and possible separate commercial licensing.

## Third-party software

Third-party components remain under their own licenses. See [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Legal note

This document is a practical explanation of the intended licensing model and is not a substitute for the authoritative license terms. Legal questions about a specific commercial use should be resolved against the actual license and, where appropriate, professional legal advice.
