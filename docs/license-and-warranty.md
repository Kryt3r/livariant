# License, Warranty & Liability

Livariant is source-available software licensed by default under the **PolyForm Perimeter License 1.0.1**. The authoritative terms are the repository `LICENSE` file.

A practical explanation of the licensing model is available in [`LICENSING.md`](../LICENSING.md).

## Source-available, not OSI Open Source

The default license permits broad use, modification, and distribution for permitted purposes, but restricts using Livariant to provide others with a product that competes with Livariant.

Because that competition restriction is part of the default license, Livariant must not be described as OSI-approved Open Source.

The intended product boundary is that individuals and organizations may use Livariant in commercial software-development work without that alone making their use competitive with Livariant. The license text controls the legal result for any specific use.

## Separate commercial licensing

The Livariant rights holder may offer separate commercial terms for uses outside the default license or for separately negotiated rights such as embedding/OEM use, enterprise arrangements, warranties, support, or other commercial permissions.

This preserves the ability to develop commercial Livariant offerings while keeping the default source-available edition broadly useful.

## Warranty and liability

PolyForm Perimeter 1.0.1 includes its own no-liability and as-is provisions. Livariant does not add a separate product warranty through the Public Preview documentation.

Public Preview support documentation describes engineering expectations for supported paths; it does not create a paid support SLA or override the `LICENSE` terms.

## Contributions

External code contributions are not yet accepted for incorporation until contributor terms compatible with Livariant's long-term licensing and commercial model are finalized.

This avoids accidentally acquiring contributions under terms that later prevent consistent source-available or separate commercial licensing. See `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `LICENSING.md`.

## Third-party material

Third-party material remains under its own license terms. The current dependency/redistribution review is summarized in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

The packed Livariant Runtime currently declares no runtime package dependencies. Build-only development dependencies remain subject to their upstream licenses.

## Product name

**Livariant** is the accepted product name. The repository's preliminary naming/collision review is documented in `distribution/product-naming-decision.md`.

That engineering review is not a formal trademark-clearance opinion. The architecture remains rename-safe if a future credible rights conflict requires a branding change.

## Legal boundary

This page is a project summary, not a replacement for the license text and not legal advice. Before materially expanding commercial licensing, contributor agreements, or enterprise distribution, the final legal documents should be reviewed by qualified counsel for the applicable jurisdiction.
